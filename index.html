Common questions

Does it work offline?

Yes, after you open it once with internet. Voice recognition in the mic mode usually needs a connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your own device, in your browser. Clearing browser data or changing phones resets it, so use the app's export option to keep a backup.

The mic does not start. What do I do?

Allow microphone access when your browser asks. The app must be opened through https. Chrome on Android works best.

Is this the official EPS-TOPIK material?

No. EPS Trainer is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with HRD Korea or any government office, and it is not sponsored by Class AI. Always check official sources for exam information.
